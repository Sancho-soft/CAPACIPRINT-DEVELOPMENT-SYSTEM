# Realistic Print-Shop UX Copy & Prototype Content

## 1. Authentic Commercial Printing Terminology
Replace generic SaaS filler ("Manage your workflow seamlessly", "Item 1") with real print-production language:
- **Substrates & Stock**: `13oz Tarpaulin`, `C2S 120gsm Gloss`, `80gsm Bond`, `Matte Vinyl Sticker`, `Sintra Board 3mm`, `DTF Transfer Film`.
- **Finishing & Pre-Press**: `Grommets / Eyelets (24in)`, `Bleed Margin (3mm)`, `Matte Lamination`, `Die-Cut Contour`, `CMYK Pre-Flight`, `Digital Proof`.
- **Machine Types**: `Solvent Roll Printer`, `UV Flatbed`, `Digital Laser Press`, `Offset Sheetfed`, `DTF & Heat Press`, `Plotter Cutter`.
- **Order Stages**: `Submitted` -> `Quotation Ready` -> `Payment Verification` -> `Branch Routed` -> `On Press` -> `Quality Check` -> `Ready for Pickup` -> `Claimed`.

## 2. Concise, Action-Oriented Microcopy
- Prefer short, scannable button labels: `Evaluate Capacity`, `Verify Specs`, `Upload Proof`, `Track Order`, `Print / Save PDF`.
- Format currency consistently as `₱` with two decimals or clean integer separators (`₱1,450.00`, `2,500 pcs`).
- Keep subtitles single-line and informative—never stack redundant marketing paragraphs above operational tables.
